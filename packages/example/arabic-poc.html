Arabic Shaping POC 01

HarfBuzz shapes Unicode Arabic into glyph IDs and positions. Three.js renders the resulting glyph outlines. This page is isolated from the current MText layout engine.

Choose an Arabic-capable TTF/OTF font first.


      

        Recommended for this first test: Arial, Tahoma, or an Arabic Noto font.
        Mixed Arabic/English BiDi is intentionally not part of POC 01; first we
        prove shaping, ligatures, marks, glyph positioning, and Three.js geometry.
